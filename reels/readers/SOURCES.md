## Specs shown (only what the VO says)
- Kobo Libra Colour: colour screen, page-turn buttons, write with a stylus. Footnote: "Kobo Stylus 2 vendu séparément" (**to confirm with City Store**).
- Kindle Colorsoft: 16GB, Black, colour screen; books, comics, magazines.
- Kindle Paperwhite 12th gen: 16GB, Black; fast, light, battery lasts weeks. Footnote: "Autonomie variable selon l'usage".
- E Ink screen that doesn't tire the eyes + water resistant: the VO says "بجوج" (both); all three models are IPX8, so the scene shows all three. Footnote: "IPX8 : jusqu'à 2 m, 60 min (eau douce)".
