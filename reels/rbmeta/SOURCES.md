# Ray-Ban Meta (3rd gen) Wayfarer Matte Black, Transitions lenses reel: sources

Official Ray-Ban | Meta renders supplied by City Store (1600 px, transparent). Originals in `assets/rbmeta/products/src/`.
The clear lenses are rendered as opaque near-white glass (white panels on the blue background), so the lens regions get a
glass alpha (whiter = more see-through; the temples seen through the lens stay visible), and the pale floor shadow is
dropped. The front view also gets a lens mask (`front_lens.png`) that the reel tints for the Transitions effect (drawn
tint over the official render, lenses only).
- USED `rbmeta_front.png` -> `front` (+ `front_lens`): hook, Transitions, camera, battery, cover.
- USED `rbmeta_angle_right.webp` -> `angle` (Ray-Ban logo on the temple = product branding): intro.
- USED `rbmeta_angle_left.webp` -> `side`: Meta AI / microphones scene.
- USED `rbmeta_box_contents.webp` -> `case` only: the charging case.
- SKIPPED `rbmeta_back.webp` (adds nothing) and, from the box-contents shot, the box and the leaflets (English print:
  "GET STARTED", "SAFETY & WARRANTY", prescription-lens leaflet) and the cloth.
The sun / house icons, the camera ring, the 12 MP / 3K badges, the button ripple and the 6 microphone dots are drawn
(the dots mark the idea of six microphones, not their exact positions).

## Voice-over
