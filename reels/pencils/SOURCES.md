# Apple Pencil range reel: sources

Official Apple renders supplied by City Store (transparent). Originals in `assets/pencils/products/src/`. All four used,
cropped and upscaled to ~940 px tall (sources: pencils 16-33 px wide, 231-632 px tall). The Apple logo printed on the
pencils is part of the official photos (allowed); no logo is drawn anywhere else. The tablets in the reel are drawn,
generic, with no logo.
Model identification was checked on the renders, not assumed from the file order:
- `pencil_pro.png` -> `pro`: "Pencil Pro" print.
- `pencil_2nd_gen.png` -> `gen2`: flat side, "Pencil" print, no cap seam.
- `pencil_1st_gen.png` -> `gen1`: round body, silver band + cap.
- `pencil_usbc.png` -> `usbc`: flat side, "Pencil" print, cap seam near the top (sliding cap over the USB-C port).

## Voice-over
36.8 s raw -> 33.1 s with pause trimming; even the max 1.10x would end the VO at 30.1 s, so per the standing rule the reel
goes over 30 s at natural speed: 36.5 s.

## Specs shown (only what the VO says)
- Hook: which one fits your iPad? 4 models available.
- Pro: squeeze to change tools, barrel roll like a real pen, magnetic charging.
- 2nd gen: pressure sensitive, double tap, attaches and charges on the side of the iPad.
- 1st gen: pressure sensitive, for older iPads.
- USB-C: the cheapest, good for writing and notes, charges with a USB-C cable (footnote: "Sans sensibilité à la pression",
  the honest limitation viewers would otherwise assume).
- Every model: footnote "Compatibilité selon le modèle d'iPad".
- Trust scene in the plural ("Produits originaux"), as spoken. CTA message asks for the iPad model, as the VO does.
