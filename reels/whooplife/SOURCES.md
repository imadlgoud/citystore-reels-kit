- USED `whoop_life_mg_band.png` -> `life`: the WHOOP Life sensor on its band (sensor side, green LEDs), enhanced 3x.
- USED `whoop_life_box_contents.png` -> `box`: only the box front ("WHOOP" + "LIFE" = packaging), enhanced 3x.
  SKIPPED from that image: the box back (small English print) and the band / cable (25 and 12 px wide).

The screen cross, pulse line, generic watch icon, activity tiles, ECG card, blood-pressure trend card and WHOOP Age dial
are drawn (French labels, no numbers presented as real readings).

goes over 30 s at natural speed: 36.5 s. The final MP4 is 30.1 MB, so `reel_9x16_upload.mp4` (crf 21) is the copy sent.
