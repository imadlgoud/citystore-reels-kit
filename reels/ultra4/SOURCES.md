- 49 mm, Titanium (natural), titanium case + sapphire screen, adventure.
- Battery up to 50 h, up to 84 h in low-power mode (footnote "Jusqu'à, selon l'usage"); hook "2 JOURS" = "يوماين".
- Precise GPS; **SOS via satellite: not available in Morocco** (Apple's supported-countries list,

- Heart, sleep and sport tracking, day and night.
- New, sealed: standard trust scene.
