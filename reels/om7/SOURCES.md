# DJI Osmo Mobile 7 reel: sources

Official DJI renders supplied by City Store (transparent, small: ~300 px). Originals in `assets/om7/products/src/`.
All used, enhanced 3x (Lanczos + unsharp + slight contrast), defringe:
- `om7_unfolded.png` -> `unfolded`: gimbal unfolded, no phone.
- `om7_folded.png` -> `folded`: gimbal folded; the arm touches the render's left edge, so that edge is feathered.
- `om7_kit_phone.png` split into its three objects:
  - `phone`: gimbal holding a phone on the camera view (official demo shot of a running dog; UI icons only, no readable text);
  - `clamp`: magnetic phone clamp;
  - `pouch`: carry pouch with the "OSMO" print (product branding; extracted, not used in the reel).
The pocket, phone back and video frames in the reel are drawn.
