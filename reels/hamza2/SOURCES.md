# Hamza & Citybot, episode 2 "الكورة قد النملة" (iPhone Duo): sources and notes

## Voice-over (supplied, not re-voiced)
`assets/hamza2/vo/voiceover.wav`: 23.61 s, 24 kHz mono, -17.9 LUFS, peak -2.9 dBFS.
- Split into L1-L8 by ffmpeg silencedetect (-35 dB, 0.2 s); L3/L4 has no silence, split at the energy dip at 9.32 s
  (confirmed by City Store). Files: `assets/hamza2/vo/lines/L1-L8.wav`, `assets/hamza2/vo/timings.json`.
- Whisper could not run (its model host is blocked in this environment): word times are estimated from the voiced
  regions weighted by syllables. Subtitle text is always the script.
- Clean-up (scripts/process_vo_take.py): gaps 0.18 s inside lines, 0.32 s between lines, natural speed, -14 LUFS;
  23.0 s of VO from 0.25 s to 23.28 s. Rhubarb (-r phonetic) per line; only the speaker lip-syncs (both on L8).
- Citybot's lines (L2, L4, L6, L7): robotic colour v2 (`reels/hamza2/robot_fx.py`): dry 70 % + a +1.5-semitone
  rubberband double (formants preserved, 9 ms, 30 %) + a static 4 ms copy (12 %), speaker EQ, de-ess; loudness-matched.
  v1 (55 Hz ring-mod + feedback comb) sounded noisy/buzzy and was replaced. L8 lighter. Clean lines kept in
  `public/hamza2/audio/vo_clean/` (and `mix_clean.wav`, v1-era clean mix).
- Music: original, generated by scripts/make_music.py (style "hamza2"); no track was supplied in assets/music/.

leagues, crests, players, broadcaster graphics or sponsors. Same match on the small phone (ball ~4 px, ant joke) and
on the unfolded Duo (big and clear).
