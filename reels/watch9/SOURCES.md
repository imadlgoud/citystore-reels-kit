# Galaxy Watch9 reel: sources

All product images were supplied by City Store (official Samsung renders, Galaxy Watch9 40 mm, Black). Originals in
`assets/watch9/products/src/`. All four were already transparent.

| File | Used for | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `watch9_40_black_angle.png` | hook (rises out of the gift box), processor scene, cover | cropped to alpha, edge defringe |
| `watch9_40_black_front.png` (386 px, small) | intro, LTE scene | enhanced (Lanczos x2.5 + unsharp + slight contrast); strap ends cut by the frame faded out |
| `watch9_40_black_back.webp` | Gemini / heart, sleep, sport scene (sensors) | cropped; the case-back engraving ("SM-L345F · 40mm · Sapphire Crystal · LTE · GPS · 5 ATM") is tiny hardware engraving, unreadable at the size used, not marketing text |
| `watch9_40_black_box_charger.webp` | charger only, small, in the battery scene | right half cropped (the watch half is a lower-quality duplicate of the front view), enhanced x1.2 |

The case-back model number SM-L345F matches a 40 mm LTE model. Watch faces show the stock dial ("SAT 28"): UI, not
marketing text. Nothing skipped.

## Specs shown (only what the VO says)
- 40 mm, LTE, Black.
- LTE: calls, answer, messages anywhere, phone left at home. Footnote: "Nécessite une eSIM / un forfait compatible".
- New Snapdragon Wear Elite processor (as in the script), fast, light on the wrist.
- Battery +20 % vs Galaxy Watch8 in the same size. Footnote: "Capacité vs Galaxy Watch8 40 mm". The size comparison
  uses drawn outlines (no Watch8 image).
- Gemini (footnote: "Gemini : connexion internet requise"); heart, sleep and sport tracking.
