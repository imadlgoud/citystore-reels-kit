## Specs shown (only what the VO says)
- 11th generation, Black (Noir), 6" E Ink screen: like paper, easy on the eyes, readable in the sun.
- 16GB: thousands of books; light in the hand.
- Built-in front light, dark mode, read at night without the room light.
- Battery up to 6 weeks (footnote: "Autonomie variable selon l'usage"), USB-C charging.
- New, sealed: standard trust scene.
