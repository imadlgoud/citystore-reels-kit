# Apple Watch Series 11 46mm reel: sources

City Store's own shop photos of the actual units (301 px each, not official renders). Originals in `assets/watch11/products/src/`.
All used images are enhanced 3x (Lanczos + unsharp + slight contrast).
- USED `w11_trio_stand.png` -> `trio`: three units on the shop stand, screens off. Background keyed out (flat grey at alpha 200).
- USED `w11_trio_setup.png` -> `setup`: three units; their screens showed the pairing screen in Spanish / Arabic / Chinese,
  so each screen (traced by hand) is filled with its own black: no text or icon remains.
- USED `w11_wrist.png` -> `wrist`: one unit on a wrist; its screen showed a language list ("English", Chinese) and is
  blanked the same way. The reel draws its own pulse line / heart / workout icon on it. The arm is cut by the photo frame,
  so the reel fades its left and right edges.
- SKIPPED `w11_back_engraving.png`: the back engraving includes the Apple logo (not allowed outside official photos).
  It does confirm the model: "SERIES 11 · 46MM ALUMINUM CASE".
- SKIPPED `w11_back_hand.png`: mostly a hand; the product is small and blurry.
No Apple logo appears anywhere in the reel. The battery and sleep scenes use a drawn generic watch (no logo, French UI).

## Specs shown (only what the VO says)
- No need for the latest model; Apple Watch Series 11, 46 mm.
- Battery up to 24 h (footnote: "Autonomie variable selon l'usage"), fast charging.
- Sleep Score every morning ("Score de sommeil"); alert when blood pressure is high (footnote: "Alertes de tension :
  pas un diagnostic médical ; disponibilité selon le pays").
- Big, strong screen; water resistant (footnote: "Résistance à l'eau non permanente, selon l'usage"); heart + workouts.
- Trust scene says exactly what the VO says: "PRODUIT ORIGINAL" + "Neuf" + "Jamais utilisé" (no "boîte fermée" claim:
  the photos show unboxed units).
