## What this means
1. **The first frame decides the reel.** Show the product and the hook text from frame 0 (`ProductHook`).
2. **Hook lines that hold people:**
   - A warning or advice: "ما تشريش … حتى تشوف هاد الفيديو", "3 حوايج خاصك تعرفهم قبل ما تشري …".
   - A comparison: "18 Pro ولا 17 Pro؟ هاهو الفرق".
   - A direct question or problem: "التيليفون ديالك كيسالي فنص النهار؟".
3. **Hook lines that get skipped:** "عندك X؟ … disponible دابا", which reads as an ad from the first second.
4. **Topics:** this audience watches phones, smartwatches, gaming and comparisons. Keep e-readers and small
   accessories for list reels ("5 cadeaux", "accessoires iPhone").
5. **Ask for comments:** a question at the end ("شنو كتختار؟") brings comments (the Oct 11 reel had 20).
6. **Shorter is fine:** 15-20 s is enough when the VO allows it. Post one reel a day, 9 pm to midnight.
