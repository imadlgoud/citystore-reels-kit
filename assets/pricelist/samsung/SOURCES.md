# Samsung availability list: sources
Transparent PNGs supplied by City Store (originals in `src/`), trimmed; small ones upscaled.
- Galaxy S26 Ultra: `galaxy_s26ultra_colours_TEXT_ON_SCREEN.png` (700 px, 4 colours + front + S Pen). The front screen
  carried a big "S26 Ultra" overlay; `clean_s26ultra.py` removes it by mirroring the screen's clean left half onto
  the right half -> `s26ultra.png` (2x). The wallpaper is therefore symmetric, not the original.
- Galaxy S25 Ultra: `galaxy_s25ultra_colours.png` (319 px, 4 titanium colours + front + S Pen) -> `s25ultra.png` (3x).
- Galaxy S26: `galaxy_s26_colours.png` (301 px, 4 colours + front) -> `s26.png` (3x).
- Galaxy A57: `galaxy_a57_colours.png` (1600 px, 4 colours + front) -> `a57.png`.
Configurations as given by City Store: S26 Ultra / S25 Ultra / S26: 12 GB / 256 GB + 12 GB / 512 GB; A57: 12 GB / 256 GB.
- Galaxy A56: `galaxy_a56_colours.png` (Graphite, Pink, Light Gray, Olive + front) -> `a56.png` (2x). 12 GB / 256 GB.
- Galaxy A36: `galaxy_a36_colours.png` (White, Lime, Lavender, Black) -> `a36.png` (2x). 12 GB / 256 GB.
