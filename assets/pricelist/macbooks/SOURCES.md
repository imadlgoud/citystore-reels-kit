# MacBook availability list: sources
Transparent PNGs supplied by City Store (originals in `src/`), trimmed; 301 px ones upscaled 3x.
- MacBook Pro M5 14" and M4 14": `macbook_pro_14_spaceblack_large.png` (1000 px, Space Black) -> `pro14.png` (same design
  for both chips; the small copy `macbook_pro_14_spaceblack_small.png` is the same render and is not used).
- MacBook Air M4 13,6": `macbook_air_13_skyblue.png` (Sky Blue) -> `air13.png`.
- MacBook Air M4 15": `macbook_air_15_midnight.png` (Midnight) -> `air15.png`.
Configurations as given by City Store: Pro M5 14" 16 GB / 1 TB + 16 GB / 512 GB; Pro M4 14" 16 GB / 1 TB + 16 GB / 512 GB;
Air M4 13,6" 16 GB / 512 GB; Air M4 15" 16 GB / 512 GB.
