- iPhone 18 Pro Max: `iphone18promax_four_colours_front.webp` (Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy + front) -> `18promax.png`
- iPhone 18 Pro: `iphone18pro_four_colours.png` (Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy) -> `18pro.png`
- iPhone 17 Pro Max: `iphone17promax_three_colours_front.webp` (Deep Blue, Silver, Cosmic Orange + front) -> `17promax.png`
- iPhone 17 Pro: `iphone17pro_front_three_colours.png` (front, Silver, Cosmic Orange, Deep Blue) -> `17pro.png`
